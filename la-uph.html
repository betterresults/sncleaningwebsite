How is a sofa cleaned at home, step by step?

First we read the care label and test the fabric, because cotton, linen, velvet, microfibre and blends each need a different approach. The sofa is vacuumed thoroughly to lift crumbs, grit and pet hair from the seams and under the cushions. Stains and heavily soiled areas on the arms, seats and headrests are pre treated, and then the fabric is cleaned with professional equipment and a cleaning solution suited to that fabric.

Cushions are cleaned on both sides where the covers allow, and the pile is brushed so it dries evenly. Most fabric sofas are dry within a few hours, depending on the fabric, the weather and the airflow in the room. We'll tell you what to expect before we leave.

Can upholstery cleaning remove pet hair, stains and odours?

Yes, in most cases. Professional upholstery cleaning lifts embedded pet hair, body oils from arms and headrests, and most food and drink stains, and treats odours at the cause rather than covering them with fragrance. Fresh stains respond best, so it's worth booking soon after a spill.

Some marks, such as ink, dyes, bleach spots or a stain already scrubbed with a shop bought product, may not come out completely, and we'll say so honestly before we start. Between cleans, vacuum the cushions weekly and blot spills with a clean white cloth rather than rubbing them.

What are the benefits of professional upholstery and sofa cleaning?

Professional sofa cleaning removes the soil and body oils that a vacuum can't reach, so the fabric looks brighter and feels softer, and it takes away the odour that builds up in cushions over the years. Regular couch cleaning also helps a good three seater last longer, because grit trapped in the fibre wears it down every time someone sits on it. Synthetic blends, microfibre and wool blends all respond well when the right method is chosen after reading the care label.

What cleaning can't fix is worth knowing too: sun fading, worn pile and a permanent dye transfer from jeans or cushions won't come out. We'll point these out before we start, so you know exactly what to expect. Drying time depends on the fabric and the ventilation in the room, and we'll give you advice for your furniture on the day.
