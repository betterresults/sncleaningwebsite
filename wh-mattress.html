How is a mattress cleaned at home, step by step?

Your cleaner looks at the label and the type of mattress first, then chooses a method to suit the mattress. Every mattress is vacuumed first with a powerful machine that reaches the seams, piping and buttons, where dust mites and dead skin collect. Stains are pre treated one by one, then the whole sleeping surface gets a sanitising clean, an odour treatment where it's needed and a dust mite treatment.

We keep moisture low so the filling never gets soaked. A mattress cleaned by hot water extraction can stay damp for hours, and on memory foam wet cleaning may damage the foam, which is why the choice between hot water extraction or the dry method matters. Some companies use a dry powder method on everything; we pick the low moisture or dry method to suit the mattress in front of us, and both sides are cleaned on a double sided mattress.

Can you remove urine, sweat and other stains from a mattress?
