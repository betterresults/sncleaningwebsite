What's the best way to clean a carpet professionally?

Every carpet type needs a slightly different approach, so your carpet cleaner checks the fibre and the level of soiling before choosing a cleaning method. After a thorough vacuum, stains and heavy traffic areas are pre treated, then the carpet is cleaned by hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, or with a low moisture method for carpets that shouldn't get too wet.

Extraction flushes the pile and pulls the dirty water straight back out, so there's no soapy residue left to attract new dirt. Odour treatments are added where needed, and the pile is groomed at the end so it dries evenly and looks fresh.

Can you get pet stains and mud out of carpet?

Most everyday marks come out or fade dramatically: mud, tea, coffee, red wine, food and the grey traffic lanes on hallways and stairs. Pet accidents get a dedicated stain and odour treatment, and the clean removes a lot of the hair, dust and allergen build up deep in the carpet.

Bleach spots, dyes, ink, or a stain already scrubbed with a shop spray may only partly fade, and we'll tell you honestly before we start. With muddy footprints, let them dry completely and vacuum them up before we arrive, as rubbing wet mud pushes it further in.

How long until the carpet is dry?

Usually a few hours. Drying times depend on the carpet, the weather and how much air is moving in the room, so open a window or keep the heating on low on cold days. Walk on it only in clean socks until it's dry and keep pets off.

Before we arrive, pick up small items and anything fragile from the floor. We'll move light furniture and clean around heavier pieces. Telling us what caused a stain helps us choose the right treatment.
