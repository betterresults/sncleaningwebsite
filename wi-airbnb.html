Can guests be asked to strip the beds and do the dishes?

Under Airbnb's current rules for hosts, if your guests pay a cleaning fee you shouldn't ask them to do tasks a cleaner would normally do, like stripping beds, washing up, starting the dishwasher or taking out rubbish. You can still ask guests to treat the home with respect in your house rules, but the changeover itself is down to you or your cleaning service.

That's why many Wickford hosts work out their cleaning fee from the cost of a professional changeover. With a clear hourly price from us, you can set a fee that covers the cleaning without putting guests off.
