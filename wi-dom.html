What will my cleaner do on each visit?

Each regular visit covers the rooms you use every day. Kitchens get worktops, the hob, the sink, splashbacks and cupboard doors cleaned, appliances wiped on the outside and bins emptied. In bathrooms we clean the toilet, basin, bath, shower and tiles, descale taps and polish mirrors. Bedrooms and living areas are dusted, including skirting boards and window sills, and floors are vacuumed and mopped throughout.

We go further than a quick tidy. Your cleaner lifts sofa cushions to vacuum underneath, moves small furniture to clean behind it and rolls back rugs to clean the floor below, the jobs that make a house feel properly clean. Extras such as changing the beds, cleaning inside the fridge or the microwave, or a bit of ironing can be built into your visits.
