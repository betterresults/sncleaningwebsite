Will a professional clean help me get my deposit back?

A professional clean is the simplest way to hand back a property as clean as it was at check in. Since the Tenant Fees Act 2019, a landlord in England can't make you pay for a professional clean, but they can deduct from your deposit if the home is less clean than at the start, beyond fair wear and tear. Dirt, grease and limescale don't count as fair wear and tear, so they're the most common reasons for a deduction.

Our deposit back guarantee covers everything related to the cleaning you booked. If the agent or landlord says an area isn't clean enough, tell us within 72 hours and we come back and clean it again free of charge. If you still disagree with a deduction, your deposit is protected by a government backed scheme (DPS, mydeposits or TDS), which deals with disputes.
