What does an after builders clean include?

An after builders clean starts with removing fine dust from the top of the room down: light fittings, door tops, frames, skirting boards, radiators, sockets and switches, then inside cupboards, drawers and wardrobes where dust settles without anyone noticing. We clean the inside of the windows, frames, sills and tracks, take off stickers and labels, and lift paint splashes and plaster spots from glass, tiles and hard floors where it's safe to do so.

Kitchens and bathrooms get special attention: grout haze and adhesive marks on new tiles, silicone smears, and dust inside the new units and appliances. Floors are vacuumed and washed, often twice, because the first pass brings up dust that was hiding in the corners. We finish with a final wipe of every surface once the air has settled, so the dust you'd otherwise see the next morning has already gone.

When should I book the clean, before or after snagging?

Book the clean when the trades have completely finished and the last coat of paint is dry, but before you move furniture back in. If snagging work is still going on, wait until it's done, or the new dust will undo the clean. A good tip is to ask your builder for the date the last trade leaves, then book us for the day after.

Let us know if the electricity and water are on, as we need both. Builders' rubbish and leftover materials should be taken away before we arrive. If you're hiring someone to remove waste, check they hold a waste carrier licence on the Environment Agency register, because if your waste is fly tipped, you can be held responsible.
