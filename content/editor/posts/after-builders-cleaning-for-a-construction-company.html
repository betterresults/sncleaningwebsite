The situation

We work with a construction company in Essex that renovates and extends family homes. Like many builders, they leave cleaning out of their quotes. Their clients are careful with money, and a full professional clean added to the price can make the difference between winning a job and losing it.

The problem comes at the end. The work is usually in one part of the home, but building dust gets everywhere: up the stairs, into the bathrooms and through doors that were kept closed. The builder knows cleaning was never part of the price, but they still don't want to hand the home back to their client dusty.

Job one: a four bedroom house in Maldon (CM9)

The house has four bedrooms, three bathrooms, a living room and a dining room. The builders had worked through the entrance, up the stairs and in the top floor rooms, and those areas, along with the toilets and bathrooms, were covered in dust. The kitchen, living room and dining room had been kept closed during the work, but some dust had still found its way in.

Instead of paying for a full clean of every room, the construction company booked two of our cleaners for a full day on a Saturday in September 2026. Emilia and Tom started with the areas the builders had used:

  • the entrance and hallway
  • the stairs and landings
  • the toilets and bathrooms
  • the top floor rooms where the work had been done

Once those were finished they still had time left, so they went through the rooms that had been closed off and dusted them and cleaned the floors.

Time and cost: two cleaners for one day, 8 hours, £460 in total. Quotes for a full after builders clean of a four bedroom, three bathroom house are often around £850.

The result: the homeowners were really happy with how clean the house was when they walked in. They had been planning to book a cleaning company themselves after the builders left, and found they didn't need to.

Job two: a large house in Ingatestone (CM4)

In November 2026 the same company finished a project on a large house in Ingatestone, near Brentwood. Again there was no cleaning in the project budget, and again they couldn't leave the home dusty for their client.

This time they booked one cleaner for a full day, at £29 an hour: 8 hours, £232. Our cleaner dusted and cleaned the areas the work had reached. It wasn't the same as a full top to bottom clean, which on a home that size would cost around £600, but the client got their home back clean and dust free in the rooms that mattered.

Why this works

When you book cleaners by the day, you decide the budget and we decide the order: the areas the builders worked in first, then everything else as far as the time allows. You pay £29 per cleaner per hour and nothing else.

Advice for builders and homeowners

  • Tell us which rooms the work touched and which were closed off. That's where the time goes first.
  • For a whole empty home, ask for the price by size instead. Everything is cleaned top to bottom for a set price.
  • If the trades are still on site, book by the day and we'll work around them.
  • Book as close to handover as you can, so the dust doesn't settle again.
