# Rules for every session in this repository

## Videos, voiceovers, music and any paid generation (Silvia's rule, 17 November 2026)

Never generate a voiceover, music, AI video, AI images or any other credit spending asset without Silvia's explicit approval first.

The order is always:
1. Write the script (spoken lines and on screen text) and show it to Silvia in the chat.
2. Wait. Do nothing else until she says it is approved. If she asks for changes, change the script and show it again.
3. Only after a clear "approved" or "go ahead" generate the voiceover, music or video.

This applies to every tool that spends credits: ElevenLabs (voice and music), Canva, fal.ai, Veo, HeyGen, Kling, Runway, Luma and any similar service. One script shown before any spend, every time, including in a brand new chat with no earlier context.

## Script quality

Recruitment and marketing scripts must be written like an experienced marketer and recruiter: open on what the person wants and who they are (a question about them, not a pay figure), build desire and belonging (join the team, help a local company grow), handle the fear, and put pay and the call to action at the end. Never open with the price or the pay. Use plain British English, no hyphens joining ordinary words, no dashes.
