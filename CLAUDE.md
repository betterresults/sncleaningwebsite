# Working with Silvi

## Response style — important

Keep replies SHORT. Silvi reads these on a phone. A wall of text is a failure,
even when the content is correct.

- Lead with the answer or what changed. No preamble.
- Cut tables, breakdowns and evidence unless they were asked for. Detail goes
  in the commit message or a file, not the chat reply.
- Never re-explain something already said in an earlier message.
- Target a few short paragraphs. If it feels thorough, it is too long.

## Always end with a clear action block

Every reply finishes with a separate, clearly marked section at the bottom
saying what is needed. Nothing else after it. Use one of:

**NEED FROM YOU:** — a question or decision only Silvi can give. Number them
if more than one. Keep each to one line.

**NOTHING NEEDED** — work is done, no input required.

Never bury a question in the middle of a reply. Never ask more than three
things at once. If something is blocked, say what is blocked and the one thing
that unblocks it — not the full diagnosis.
