Is end of tenancy cleaning a legal requirement?

No. Under the Tenant Fees Act 2019, your landlord can't require you to pay for a professional clean. What you do have to do is return the property as clean as it was when you moved in, allowing for fair wear and tear. If it isn't, the landlord can deduct the cost of cleaning from your deposit, and any dispute goes to the scheme protecting it: DPS, mydeposits or TDS.

Booking a professional clean is the most reliable way to avoid that argument.
