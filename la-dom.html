A regular domestic clean includes the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, worked through room by room. How do I find a trusted domestic cleaner in Laindon?

Start by asking who will actually come into your home. Directories such as Checkatrade list individual cleaners and cleaning companies side by side, so check whether the people sent are employed and trained by the company, whether they're insured and DBS checked, and whether the same cleaner comes each time. A good cleaning company should also tell you what's included in a visit and what happens if something is missed.
