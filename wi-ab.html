What's included in a builders clean?

We work from the top of each room down, removing fine dust from light fittings, door tops, frames, skirting boards, radiators and sockets, then inside cupboards, drawers and wardrobes. Cleaning windows on the inside is part of the job: glass, frames, sills and tracks, with stickers and labels removed. Paint splashes and plaster spots are lifted from glass, tiles and hard floors wherever it's safe.

In kitchens and bathrooms we clear grout haze and adhesive from new tiles, silicone smears and dust inside new units and appliances. Floors are vacuumed and washed, usually twice, because dust keeps settling out of the air, and every surface gets a final wipe before we leave.

Should I book the clean before or after snagging?

After. Book the clean once every trade has finished and the paint is dry, but before furniture and carpets go back in. If snagging repairs are still to come, wait for them, or the new dust will undo the work. Asking your builder for the date the last trade leaves makes it easy to book us for the next day.

We need electricity and water on. Builders' waste should be cleared before we arrive; if you're paying someone to take it away, check their waste carrier licence on the Environment Agency register, because you can be held responsible if your waste is fly tipped.
